Heading five – engraved label

A good bookshelf says a lot about its owner. The spines face outward, and so do the owner's interests: here a well-thumbed field guide, there a cookbook with the corners turned. Body copy is set in Lora at a comfortable measure, with true italics, highlighted passages, N.B. abbreviations and inline code.

Keyboard shortcuts appear as Ctrl + S; small print reads in small capitals; struck words and inserted words use the oxblood and hunter dyes.
